Privacy Policy

Franchise 101 Growth Partners LLP, operating under the brand name “Franchise 101” (“Franchise 101”, “we”, “us” or “our”), respects the privacy of visitors, investors, entrepreneurs, franchise brands, business owners and other users interacting with our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, process, store and share personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, book a consultation, interact with our advertisements, communicate with us through WhatsApp, email or telephone, or otherwise engage with Franchise 101.

We handle personal data in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable rules and regulations as brought into force from time to time.

1. Information We May Collect

Personal and Contact Information

  • Name
  • Mobile number and WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • City, State and preferred business location

Investor / Franchise Requirement Information

  • Approximate investment budget
  • Preferred franchise or business category
  • Preferred investment location
  • Business or professional experience
  • Desired level of operational involvement
  • Existing commercial space/location, if any
  • Expected investment timeline and business objectives
  • Any additional information voluntarily provided during consultation

Brand / Business Information

Where you approach us as a brand, entrepreneur or existing business:

  • Business/brand name, website and social-media details
  • Nature/category of business, outlet count and locations
  • Business-development, franchise expansion, and marketing requirements

Technical and Usage Information

  • IP address, browser/device information, referral sources, and conversion analytics.

2. How We May Collect Information

Information may be collected through website enquiry forms, “Find the Right Franchise” questionnaires, consultation booking forms, WhatsApp Business, telephone calls, email communication, Meta/Google advertising lead forms, and direct offline meetings.

3. How We May Use Your Information

  • Understand your business, franchise or investment requirements
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry and schedule consultations
  • Identify franchise opportunities aligned with your stated criteria
  • Facilitate direct introductions with relevant franchise brands
  • Assist with business-development, branding, marketing, outlet-development or franchise expansion
  • Prevent fraud, misuse or security incidents

4. Sharing of Information

Where reasonably required for providing our services, information may be shared with relevant franchise brands in connection with an investor enquiry, execution partners, CRM/technology providers, and professional legal/financial advisors. We do not sell personal information to third-party data brokers.

5. Data Security & Retention

Franchise 101 implements technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards to protect personal data. Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill business advisory, compliance, and dispute resolution requirements.
