Before you apply

Straight answers on fees, legal & setup.

Anything not covered here, ask on the application form — a dedicated franchise manager replies within 24 hours.

Nothing. Food brands pay our marketplace facilitation fee when a verified agreement is signed. You never pay a shortlisting, consultation, or registration fee — if anyone asks for one in our name, report it immediately.

Listed ranges cover brand fee, kitchen equipment, furniture, branding and fit-out. Commercial rent deposit, local municipal licences (FSSAI, GST, Fire), and about 3 to 6 months of working capital sit on top. We send a line-by-line capex sheet with every shortlist so there are zero surprises.

In FOFO (Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated), you own and personally run the day-to-day business — you retain all profits after paying a 4-8% brand royalty. In FOCO (Franchise Owned, Company Operated), you invest the capital, but the brand handles staffing, inventory and daily operations, paying you a guaranteed minimum guarantee (MG) or 15-22% gross revenue share.

Not for kiosk, beverage, and standard QSR formats — brands supply central gravies, frozen bases, and pre-mixes with strict SOPs that can be run by 10th-pass staff after 7 days of training. Fine dining and territory rights do require past retail/management experience.

Yes. If you already have a commercial shutter, the brand's territory team surveys footfall and approves the location. If you do not have space, our local real estate partners help identify viable 100–500 sqft spots near colleges, high streets, tech parks, or transit junctions.

Typically 4 to 6 weeks for kiosks, 6 to 9 weeks for QSRs, and 12 to 16 weeks for casual dining restaurants with extensive civil work and liquor licensing.
