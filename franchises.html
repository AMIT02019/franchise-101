By Budget Bracket

What ₹6 Lakh Buys, and What ₹60 Lakh Does.

Pick the bracket you can fund without borrowing against the house. The format follows the capital, not the other way round.

₹6–15L

Kiosk & Takeaway Formats. Low capex, high density, quick to launch.

  • Chai, momo, waffle and bakery counters
  • 90–200 sqft, shutter or mall kiosk
  • Break-even typically 11–15 months
  • Can run hands-on or with compact 2-person crew
₹15–30L

Full QSR Outlets. Dine-in counter with a dedicated commercial kitchen line.

  • Ice cream parlours, burger and biryani QSR
  • 300–600 sqft on a high-street location
  • Break-even typically 16–22 months
  • Needs a shift manager and 4–6 staff
₹30–60L

Cafés & Casual Dining. Higher ticket size, premium seating, higher repeat footfall.

  • Café lounges and 40–80 cover restaurants
  • 1,000–2,000 sqft with roadside visibility
  • Break-even typically 20–28 months
  • Six months of working capital is recommended
₹60L+

Master Territory Rights. Exclusive rights for a city, district or state.

  • Territory exclusivity with sub-franchise royalty sharing
  • Three to five units committed over 24 months
  • Direct founder introductions and area exclusivity
  • Centralized regional supply coordination
