Execution Roadmap

Our 6-Week Franchise Development Process

Week 1

Recipe Audit & Yield Testing

Our culinary team audits your kitchen, records gram-level ingredient ratios, and tests frozen/pre-mix shelf life stability.

Week 2

Operations Manual & Station SOPs

We author the 120-page Brand Operations Bible covering food safety, opening/closing checklists, staff training, and customer service.

Week 3

Kitchen CAD & Equipment Vendor Ties

We finalize stainless fabrication blueprints, POS integration, and negotiate institutional equipment discounts for your future franchisees.

Week 4

Legal Franchise Agreement Drafting

Our legal partners draft your 5-year territorial license, royalty fee clauses, termination terms, and non-compete covenants.

Week 5-6

Pitch Deck, Video Assets & Marketplace Launch

We produce cinematic food photography, founder pitch video, capex spreadsheets, and launch your brand live on Franchise 101.

Result: Rapidly expanding zero-royalty burger chain with 16+ yrs manufacturing backbone.
Pure Vegetarian Casual Dining

Veg Sutra
