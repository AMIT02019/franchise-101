OUR PROCESS

From Idea to Opening Day & Scale

Understand → Conceptualise → Develop → Design → Build → Launch → Scale

01 🔍

Discovery & Requirement Study

Understand the idea, product, target customer, investment, business objectives and intended scale.

02 📐

Concept & Business Structure

Develop the concept, positioning, format, product/menu direction and broad business model.

03 🎨

Brand & Experience Development

Develop the identity, visual language, customer experience and relevant brand assets.

04 📋

Operational Development

Develop menu/product systems, SOPs, kitchen requirements, vendors, training and operating processes as applicable.

05 🏗️

Outlet Development

Architecture, space planning, interiors, fit-out and physical brand execution where required.

06 📣

Go-to-Market

Digital presence, content, social media, campaigns and launch planning.

07 🚀

Launch

Coordinate relevant stakeholders towards operational readiness and launch.

08 📈

Prepare to Scale

For suitable businesses, develop the systems, commercial structure and franchise proposition required to explore expansion.
