THE 8-STAGE JOURNEY

From Idea to Opening Day & Scale

Understand → Conceptualise → Develop → Design → Build → Launch → Scale

STAGE 01

Discovery & Requirement Study

Understand the idea, product, target customer, investment capacity, business objectives and intended scale.

STAGE 02

Concept & Business Structure

Develop the concept, market positioning, format, product/menu direction and broad commercial model.

STAGE 03

Brand & Experience Development

Develop brand identity, naming, visual language, packaging, customer experience and digital touchpoints.

STAGE 04

Operational Development

Develop recipe standardisation, culinary SOPs, kitchen layouts, vendor tie-ups and staff training frameworks.

STAGE 05

Outlet Development

Architecture, space planning, MEP drawings, interior design, equipment installation and physical fit-out execution.

STAGE 06

Go-to-Market

Digital presence setup, launch campaigns, content production, social media activation and footfall drivers.

STAGE 07

Launch

Coordinate all stakeholders, conduct dry runs, ensure regulatory compliance, and execute grand opening.

STAGE 08

Prepare to Scale

For suitable businesses, develop franchise documentation, commercial models and growth funnels for expansion.
