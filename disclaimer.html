1. Franchise & Business Opportunity Disclaimer

Franchise opportunities and business ventures involve inherent commercial and financial risk. Franchise 101 provides consulting, facilitation, opportunity discovery and business-support services and does not guarantee the profitability, operational performance, or commercial success of any franchise or business.

Investors and entrepreneurs are strongly encouraged to independently evaluate each opportunity, review all relevant franchise disclosure documents (FDD) and legal agreements, verify commercial claims directly with the respective franchisor, conduct rigorous ground-level due diligence, and obtain independent legal, financial, and tax counsel prior to executing agreements or committing investment capital.

Any franchise matching, recommendation, brand shortlist, or introductory consultation conducted by Franchise 101 is formulated based on available market information and stated investor criteria. It should not be construed as an endorsement, guarantee, or financial underwriting of future business performance.

Compact Summary (For Franchise Cards & Enquiry Forms):
“Franchise investments involve business risk. Information displayed is for general and consultation purposes and should not be treated as a guarantee of returns or profitability. Investors should independently evaluate the opportunity and relevant agreements before investing.”

2. Financial Information & ROI Disclaimer

Any investment figure, setup cost, projected revenue, average sales figure, gross margin, net margin, break-even period, payback timeline, Return on Investment (ROI), or projected profitability displayed on this website or communicated by Franchise 101 representatives is provided for general informational and exploratory evaluation purposes only.

Such information may comprise estimates, forward-looking projections, illustrative model calculations, historical performance records, brand-provided claims, or operational assumptions. Actual business performance can and will vary materially depending on critical local factors including:

  • Commercial real estate location, catchment footfall, and monthly rental terms
  • Store size, frontage, layout efficiency, and local licensing
  • Manpower availability, wage inflation, and operational management discipline
  • Raw material procurement costs, supply chain disruptions, and wastage control
  • Hyperlocal competition, pricing sensitivity, and shifting consumer preferences
  • Seasonal fluctuations, macroeconomic conditions, and local municipal regulations
No Guaranteed Returns: Past performance, historical outlet results, or brand projections do not guarantee future performance. Franchise 101 does not guarantee any specific level of revenue, gross/net profit, return percentage, break-even milestone, or payback timeline. Investors must verify all financial models with the relevant franchisor and licensed financial advisors.

Compact Table / Card Disclaimer: “Financial figures are indicative and may be based on information supplied by the respective brand. Actual results may vary. No returns or profitability are guaranteed.”

3. Third-Party Franchise Brand Information

Franchise brand names, trademarks, logos, registered marks, photographs, menu designs, and brand-related intellectual property showcased on Franchise 101 belong to independent third-party businesses and franchisors.

Unless expressly stated in writing, such brands remain independently owned, managed, and operated. They are not subsidiaries, joint ventures, or corporate affiliates of Franchise 101 Growth Partners LLP.

Information regarding investment requirements, franchise fees, royalty percentages, active outlet counts, master expansion territories, and franchisor support packages is provided or approved by the respective brand and is subject to alteration without notice. Franchise 101 endeavours to display information responsibly, but users must confirm all material data with the franchisor directly before entering commercial commitments.
