Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, objective insights into the Franchise 101 ecosystem.

What does Franchise 101 do?

Franchise 101 is a franchise and business solutions ecosystem helping investors discover and evaluate franchise opportunities, entrepreneurs develop businesses from concept to launch, and existing brands strengthen, franchise and scale.

How does Franchise 101 help me find a franchise?

We begin by understanding your investment capacity, preferred location, business interests, timeline and desired level of involvement. Based on these requirements, our team identifies relevant opportunities from our network and supports the investor through consultation, brand introductions and evaluation.

Does Franchise 101 guarantee returns?

No. Every business involves risk and performance depends on factors including location, operations, management, market conditions and brand performance. Franchise 101 assists with opportunity discovery and evaluation but does not guarantee revenue, profitability, returns or business success.

Are the franchises on the website the only brands available?

The website showcases selected opportunities currently represented or available through our ecosystem. Our team can discuss relevant opportunities based on the investor’s stated requirements and current availability.

Can Franchise 101 help me set up my outlet?

Yes. Depending on the franchise and requirement, our wider business-development ecosystem may support areas including location assistance, outlet planning, design, fit-out, kitchen development, operational setup, branding, marketing and launch coordination.

Can Franchise 101 help me create my own brand?

Yes. Our Build Your Brand services are designed for entrepreneurs wanting to develop their own business concept rather than purchase an existing franchise.

I already own a business. Can Franchise 101 help me franchise it?

Yes. Our Franchise & Scale services support existing businesses looking to strengthen their proposition, prepare for franchising, acquire investors and expand.

Do you also provide franchise marketing?

Yes. Our Franchise Marketing services support brands with investor-facing positioning, campaign development, digital/performance marketing, content, lead generation and lead qualification.

Is consultation paid?

Our initial consultation is designed to understand your requirements and discuss appropriate opportunities or services. Share your details through our consultation form to get started.

How do I get started?

Explore our opportunities, complete the Find the Right Franchise questionnaire, WhatsApp us or book a consultation.
