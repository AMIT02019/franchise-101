Our Origin

Why Franchise 101 Was Created

Franchise 101 was created from a simple observation: the franchise ecosystem needs greater trust, clarity and alignment between investors and brands.

Investors can commit significant capital to business opportunities without always having enough guidance to determine whether a particular opportunity genuinely suits their requirements. At the same time, responsible brands looking to expand need access to investors who understand their business and are appropriately aligned with their model.

Through our partners’ combined experience across franchising, restaurant development, business consulting, production, design and execution, we identified an opportunity to build something broader than a traditional franchise consultancy.

The Problem We Aim to Solve

Reduce Confusion. Improve Fit. Build Trust.

For Investors: We make the franchise journey structured by understanding requirements, filtering relevant opportunities, facilitating informed brand conversations and supporting setup where required.

For Entrepreneurs: We eliminate the complexity of coordinating numerous disconnected consultants, agencies, vendors and specialists by connecting complementary business-development capabilities through one ecosystem.

For Brands: We combine franchise development, marketing and investor acquisition into a unified, integrated approach to expansion.

Core Definition

What “Curated & Trusted” Means

Curated does not mean risk-free or guaranteed. It means Franchise 101 aims to understand the franchise opportunities it works with, assess the available proposition and present relevant opportunities based on investor requirements rather than simply functioning as an unrestricted franchise listing directory.

✓ Transparent Communication

No hidden conditions or disguised commercial interests.

✓ Responsible Representation

Vetting concepts before introducing them to prospective investors.

✓ Clear Disclaimers

Acknowledging commercial and business investment risk explicitly.

✓ Genuine Case Studies

✓ Appropriate Matching

Tailoring opportunities to investor capital, location, and involvement.

✓ No Guaranteed Returns

Refusing misleading financial promises or artificial ROI projections.

Our Approach

How We Guide Decisions

Understand the Investor → Understand the Opportunity → Identify the Fit → Facilitate Evaluation → Support the Journey

01 · Understand

Investor Profiling

We study investment capacity, location, interests, experience, timeline and management involvement.

02 · Curate

Opportunity Assessment

We develop a deep understanding of the franchise opportunities within our network, including their concept, Capex, operating model and expansion objectives.

03 · Match

Precision Shortlisting

We identify opportunities that align closely with the investor's stated budget, city territory, and operational goals.

Transparent Diligence
