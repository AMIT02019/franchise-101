Our Brand Story

Why Franchise 101 Was Started

For decades, India's franchise brokerage industry was broken. Legacy portals acted as paid banner directories, promoting any brand willing to pay listing fees without auditing store-level P&L, verifying chef-less SOPs, or checking whether early franchisees actually made money.

We created Franchise 101 to fix this information asymmetry. We operate as an institutional verification layer. Every food franchise listed on our platform undergoes rigorous scrutiny: 35%+ minimum gross margin verification, supplier invoice audits, 500m radius territorial exclusivity, and 100% turnkey operational training.
