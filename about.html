BRAND STORY

Why Franchise 101 Was Started

Franchise 101 was born from a simple observation: the franchise ecosystem needs greater trust, clarity and alignment between investors and brands.

Investors can commit substantial savings and capital to businesses without always receiving the guidance required to determine whether an opportunity is genuinely suited to them. At the same time, responsible brands looking to scale need access to investors who understand their business and are aligned with their requirements.

Through our partners' combined experience across franchising, restaurant consulting, business development, production and design, we saw an opportunity to build something broader—a platform that understands both sides of the relationship, helping the right investors meet the right opportunities.

Problem We Aim to Solve: Reduce Confusion. Improve Fit. Build Trust.

We aim to make the franchise journey more structured by understanding investor requirements, filtering relevant opportunities, and supporting the journey beyond selection. For entrepreneurs and businesses, we reduce the complexity of dealing with multiple disconnected consultants, agencies and vendors by bringing complementary business-development capabilities together through one ecosystem.

THE FRANCHISE 101 ADVANTAGE

Why Franchise 101

An investor-first philosophy combined with practical execution experience across every stage of the business journey.

1. Investor-First Understanding

We start with the investor's requirements, objectives and capacity before recommending an opportunity.

2. Curated Opportunities

Rather than a directory of every available franchise, our objective is to build a curated pool of brands that our team can understand and represent responsibly.

3. Practical Industry Experience

Our team brings experience across franchising, restaurant development, business setup, design, marketing and expansion.

4. Beyond Franchise Selection

Our wider ecosystem enables us to support entrepreneurs beyond choosing a brand, including business and outlet development where required.

5. Support for Both Sides

We understand the requirements of investors as well as the growth objectives of franchise brands, helping us facilitate better-aligned conversations.

6. One Ecosystem, Multiple Capabilities

From finding a franchise to building a new concept or scaling an existing brand, Franchise 101 is built around multiple stages of the business journey.

💡
Meaning of “Curated & Trusted”

“Curated does not mean risk-free or guaranteed. It means Franchise 101 aims to understand the brands it works with, assess their proposition and present relevant opportunities based on investor requirements rather than operating as an unrestricted listing directory.”

OUR METHODOLOGY

Our Approach

Understand → Curate → Match → Evaluate → Support

01. UNDERSTAND Study Goals

We study investment capacity, location, interests, experience, timeline and management involvement.

02. CURATE Evaluate Brands

We understand our brand network's concepts, requirements, operating models and expansion objectives.

03. MATCH Filter Opportunities

We identify opportunities that align closely with the investor's stated requirements.

04. EVALUATE Direct Dialogue

We facilitate investor-brand conversations so questions, commercials and expectations are thoroughly vetted.

05. SUPPORT Full Execution

Where required, our wider ecosystem assists with subsequent business development and launch needs.
