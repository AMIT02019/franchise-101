EXPANSION FRAMEWORK

The 6-Stage Expansion Journey

Assess → Strengthen → Systemise → Franchise → Market → Expand

STAGE 01 🔍

Assess

Understand the existing business, current unit economics, customer loyalty and expansion objectives. We perform a rigorous operational diagnostic of your current outlets, unit-level profitability, revenue stability, and baseline scalability before commercial expansion.

STAGE 02 📐

Strengthen

Identify areas of the brand, proposition, packaging and customer experience requiring improvement. Refining store aesthetics, brand touchpoints, packaging, menu/product mix, and service consistency to elevate brand equity and defensibility.

STAGE 03 📋

Systemise

Develop repeatable store processes, training frameworks, kitchen/service manuals and operating SOPs. Creating clear standard operating manuals, opening/closing checklists, recipe/product cards, and staff training modules to ensure zero founder dependency.

STAGE 04 🤝

Franchise Development

Structure the commercial model, franchise fee, royalty framework, agreements and investor presentation. Engineering unit economics with 25–35% target franchisee ROI, crafting equitable royalty structures, master territory definitions, and franchise agreements.

STAGE 05 📣

Go to Market

Position and market the franchise opportunity to targeted, qualified investor audiences. Deploying multi-channel digital campaigns, investor portal listings, financial teardowns, and targeted discovery webinars to generate high-intent investor interest.

STAGE 06 🚀

Investor Acquisition & Expansion

Generate, qualify and facilitate relevant investor opportunities as the brand scales across territories. Conducting financial screening, lead consultations, discovery sessions, commercial agreements, and hands-on rollout coordination for rapid territory expansion.
