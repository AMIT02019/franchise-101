EXPANSION FRAMEWORK

The 6-Stage Expansion Journey

Assess → Strengthen → Systemise → Franchise → Market → Expand

STAGE 01

Assess

Understand the existing business, current unit economics, customer loyalty and expansion objectives.

STAGE 02

Strengthen

Identify areas of the brand, proposition, packaging and customer experience requiring improvement.

STAGE 03

Systemise

Develop repeatable store processes, training frameworks, kitchen manuals and operating SOPs.

STAGE 04

Franchise Development

Structure the commercial model, franchise fee, royalty framework, agreements and investor presentation.

STAGE 05

Go to Market

Position and market the franchise opportunity to targeted, qualified investor audiences.

STAGE 06

Investor Acquisition & Expansion

Generate, qualify and facilitate relevant investor opportunities as the brand scales across territories.
